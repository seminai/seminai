# Developer Certificate of Origin

Seminai uses Developer Certificate of Origin version 1.1. By contributing, you certify
that the contribution was created by you, is based on work you are entitled to submit, or
was provided to you under an appropriate open-source licence; and that you understand the
contribution and its sign-off are public records if the project is published.

Add a sign-off to every contribution:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Use `git commit --signoff`. The canonical DCO text is maintained by the Linux Foundation at
<https://developercertificate.org/>.

Contributions are provided under the repository's AGPL-3.0-or-later terms. Contributors also
grant the copyright holder permission to include their contribution in separately negotiated
commercially licensed distributions of Seminai.
